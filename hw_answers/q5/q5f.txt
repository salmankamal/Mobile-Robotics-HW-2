Before calibration, the robot suffered from two major systematic errors: a track-width mismatch (b) that caused a turn overshoot, and a wheel radius mismatch (rL not equal to rR) that caused a leg curvature. These systematic errors caused the square trajectory to distort and drift by tens of centimeters. Correcting b and individual radii via UMBmark and the scale test effectively reduced these systematic biases to zero.

Because all deterministic geometric biases have been removed, the remaining error is caused entirely by non-systematic physical simulation dynamics that kinematic dead reckoning cannot model such as wheel acceleration skid and inertial slip, drag and frictional forces, and controller thresholds in umbmark_driver.py. This residual of less than a cm over an 8m total perimeter confirms that the UMBmark and scale calibration procedure successfully tuned the odometry model to the physical limits of the vehicle.
